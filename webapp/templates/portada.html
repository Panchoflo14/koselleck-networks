koselleck-networks

Did the Sattelzeit reorganize the vocabulary as a system?

Reinhart Koselleck argued that 1770-1830 was not just an era when many words changed meaning independently, but a moment when the conceptual vocabulary of the language reorganized together, as a system. This tool lets you explore the same underlying data - words and how closely they cluster with each other, period by period - through two different interfaces. We're not yet sure which one is more useful for a historian's own workflow, so both are here.

Graph explorer

See the network

A word and its closest neighbours drawn as a network diagram. Move the period slider to watch the neighbourhood - and the wider community structure - reorganize across time. Best for getting an intuitive, visual sense of how a whole cluster of words moves together.

 Word search

Look up a word

Type a word and get back a plain list: its closest neighbours by similarity, its community, and whether that community changed since the period before. No diagram to read - closer to looking a word up in a period dictionary or thesaurus.
